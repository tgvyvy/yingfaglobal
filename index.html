MARKET ENTRY CONSULTING

Business Setup & Market Entry in Vietnam

For international businesses looking to explore the Vietnamese market, build local relationships, identify business opportunities, or establish a long-term presence, we support the appropriate market-entry structure and initial setup process.

Representative Office — Establish a Local Presence

For international businesses looking to explore the Vietnamese market, build local relationships, and identify business opportunities before committing to full-scale operations, a Representative Office can provide a practical starting point.

Key advantages

  • Establish a local presence in Vietnam
  • Conduct market research and develop business relationships
  • Explore potential partners and market opportunities
  • Relatively simple structure with lower operating requirements

Our support

  • Market-entry and setup consultation
  • Preparation and submission of registration documents
  • Liaison with relevant authorities
  • Assistance with local staffing and initial administrative setup

Typical timeline

From 15–20 working days, depending on the specific case and applicable regulations.

Small Service Company — Build and Operate Your Business

For businesses ready to provide services, enter into contracts, and generate revenue in Vietnam, establishing a local company provides a more suitable foundation for long-term operations.

We support businesses across eligible sectors, including consulting, IT, marketing, professional services, trading, and other business activities.

Key advantages

  • Establish a fully registered local business entity
  • Enter into contracts and invoice customers in Vietnam
  • Conduct business operations and generate local revenue
  • Build a foundation for long-term growth in Vietnam

Our support

  • Business structure and market-entry advisory
  • Capital and ownership structure consultation
  • Preparation of corporate documents and charter
  • Support with Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC)
  • Business-line and regulatory compliance guidance
Important:

Business activities, ownership structures, licensing requirements, and tax obligations vary depending on the nature and scope of the investment. We help clients identify the appropriate structure and compliance requirements before proceeding.
